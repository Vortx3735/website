This year, our team decided to name our robot Noah's Ark in honor of our alumnus Noah Lewis, who passed away in the fall of 2025. While he was on the team, Noah lived out our mission by encouraging his teammates and taking the time to light a spark in not just his teammates but everyone around him. Noah started his journey in 2021 and rose through the ranks of the team with his expanding knowledge and his warm and welcoming presence. He dedicated his heart and soul to the team and made us smile even in our darkest moments, always showing up and being present for the team in ways we didn't know we needed. Though his presence is deeply missed, we carry on his legacy by emulating his positive energy and can-do attitude. This year we dedicated our hearts and souls to making this season our best, in honor of Noah. We hope you're watching proudly, Noah!
