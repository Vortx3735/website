Our journey began in 2011, when a group of like-minded students founded FRC Team 3735 as “Klein Bots.” Little did they know that their passion and dedication would drive the team to where we are today.

Through 14 seasons (as of 2025) we have won 1 Impact Award, made 4 Worlds appearances, earned 10 judged awards and won 1 event. See every [robot](/robots/) and [award](/awards/).
