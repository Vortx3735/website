## When are the meetings?

**Offseason (April through December):** every Friday, 3:30 to 7:30 pm.

**Season (January through April):** Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 3:30 to 7:30 pm, and Saturday, 9 am to 2 pm.

Meetings can change, but we announce changes in advance. See the [calendar](/calendar/).

## Where are the meetings?

In the Klein High garage: Klein High School, 16715 Stuebner Airline Rd, Spring, TX 77379.
