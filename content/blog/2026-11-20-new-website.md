We're excited to officially launch the brand-new online home for FRC Team 3735, VorTX!

As a student-led FIRST Robotics Competition team representing all five Klein ISD high schools, we put a lot of work into designing, building, and programming our robots each season. Now, we have a fresh, updated website to share everything our team is up to - built from the ground up by Anurag, Rohan, and our Business Team.

Whether you're a student looking to join, a sponsor supporting our program, or family and friends keeping up with our season, there's plenty to check out:

**• Explore Our Robots:** Head over to the Robots tab to take a look at our history on the field - from past machines like Tempest, Mantis, Guillotine, and Bermuda to Noah's Ark, built in honor of our late teammate and alumnus, Noah Lewis.

**• Browse the Gallery:** Check out our updated photo gallery featuring behind-the-scenes build sessions in the shop, competition highlights, and community outreach events.

**• Season Updates & Events:** Stay up to date on our FIRST In Texas district competitions, offseason events, and annual summer STEM camps.

**• Get Involved & Support:** Find information on how Klein ISD CTE students can join the team, or learn how local businesses and sponsors can partner with us.

A huge thank you to Anurag, Rohan, and the Business Team for putting together a clean, easy-to-navigate space for us to share our progress and connect with our community. Take a few minutes to look around, check out the gallery, and explore the robots we've built over the years.
Be sure to bookmark the site and follow us on Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube for the latest updates from the shop!
